Welcome!

About Me

I am a software engineer based out of Colorado. I have two degrees—a BS in Statistics and a BA in Philosophy—one of which I use slightly more than the other. If you are interested in the details of my career, please visit my LinkedIn account, linked at the bottom of this page.

Interests

In my free time, I like to run, play board games, and read.

I have a particular fascination with sticking to a CLI-based workflow. I find that this allows me to be flexible with the OS I run, as well as having knock-on benefits in encouraging scripting and automation.

What I Do

I've spent the majority of my relatively short career dealing with data. I've worked with structured data, image data, email text data, and some custom binary data, to name a few. My data work at my most recent job has mostly been to support machine learning applications.

Recently, I (and I'm sure many others) have been exploring the capabilities of AI agents. I've liked playing around with Google's ADK in particular. I'm currently working on an agent-focused product.
